## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-performance clinical environment calibrated for elite aesthetic medical practices, cosmetic dermatologists, and bespoke surgical suites. The brand ethos balances clinical certitude with the tactile indulgence of a high-end atelier. 

The aesthetic diverges sharply from cold utilitarian hospital software and generic corporate healthcare blues. Instead, it leverages an architectural editorial minimalism: calm alabaster canvases, deep midnight slate typography, and surgical iris-violet indicators that highlight complex treatment charting, skin metric diagnostics, and high-ticket procedure tracking. 

Key attributes:
- **Quiet Authority:** Authoritative typography paired with generous whitespace communicates absolute safety, surgical precision, and luxury service.
- **Material Restraint:** Translucent surfaces, sub-millimeter borders, and optical shadows establish tactile depth without visual noise.
- **Editorial Clinicalism:** Data density remains balanced; high-resolution imaging, patient histories, and procedural schedules read with the elegance of an art monograph.

## Colors

The palette rejects standard medical cyan and sterile cool-grays, opting for deep midnight tones and warm mineral underpinnings.

- **Primary (`#1E1B4B` / `#312E81`):** Deep Midnight Slate. Used for primary typography, dominant structural headers, key navigation anchors, and authoritative clinical summaries. It grounds the UI with weight and discretion.
- **Secondary (`#6366F1` / Iris Violet):** The operational accent. Indicates active selection states, key interaction surfaces, injection mapping tools, and clinical focus indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#10B981` / `#059669` - Sage / Eucalyptus):** Reserved for clinical verification, completed treatment cycles, and positive account reconciliations. It provides an organic, calming validation rather than an alarming neon success state.
- **Champagne Amber (`#D97706` / `#F59E0B`):** Employed for pending balances, scheduled procedure holds, sensitive allergy warnings, and consent verification queues.
- **Canvas & Surface Neutral (`#FBFBFD` to `#F3F4F8`):** Layered porcelain and warm alabaster. These shades create gentle separation without jarring contrasts, reducing retinal fatigue during prolonged charting sessions.
- **Borders & Separators:** Optical boundaries defined by low-opacity midnight slate (`rgba(30, 27, 75, 0.06)`) and frosted highlights (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`).

## Typography

Typographic execution relies entirely on **Plus Jakarta Sans**, utilizing its geometric clarity, tall x-height, and sculpted apertures to maintain crisp legibility in both large diagnostic displays and tight tabular record views.

- **Tabular Numerics:** All medical quantities, volumetric measurements (e.g., cc, units), pricing totals, and appointment timestamps must enforce `font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1, "cv01" 1` to ensure absolute vertical scan alignment across patient ledgers.
- **Micro-Badges & Metadata:** Set in `label-sm`, all-caps, with `letterSpacing: 0.05em` and bold weights. This ensures immediate identification of clinical flags without overpowering body copy.
- **Rhythm & Hierarchy:** Clinical notes use `body-lg` at 1.6 line-height for uninterrupted readability, while data-heavy sidebars collapse to `body-md` and `body-sm`.

## Layout & Spacing

The workstation uses an asymmetric, high-density split canvas engineered around practitioner workflows:
- **Workstation Structure:** A 16-column flexible layout engine for high-resolution diagnostic panels (minimum breakpoint `1440px`), collapsing to a responsive 12-column layout for desktop monitors and an 8-column layout for clinical tablet devices (`768px` to `1024px`).
- **Sidebar & Panels:** Clinical utilities (such as anatomical facial charts and syringe dosing rails) sit in dedicated modular docks with fixed widths (`380px` desktop) paired with fluid multi-column patient history streams.
- **Spacing Scale:** Built on a disciplined 4px base rhythm. Small intervals (`space-xs`, `space-sm`) structure dense metric matrices and dosage inputs; broader intervals (`space-lg`, `space-xl`) isolate distinct treatment episodes and diagnostic photographic galleries.
- **Margins & Safe Zones:** Canvas boundaries maintain `margin-lg` (40px) padding to prevent visual crowding around edge-mounted medical device telemetry and navigation hubs.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through translucent structural planes, crisp low-contrast borders, and deeply feathered, slate-tinted ambient shadows rather than stark mechanical drop-shadows.

- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Solid warm alabaster (`#FBFBFD`). The primary physical layer on which all diagnostic panels rest.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Surfaces):** Solid pure porcelain (`#FFFFFF`) framed by an optical sub-border of `1px solid rgba(30, 27, 75, 0.05)`. Casts an ambient shadow: `0 2px 12px -2px rgba(30, 27, 75, 0.03), 0 1px 3px rgba(30, 27, 75, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Flyouts & Diagnostic Modals):** Frosted porcelain backdrop (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`). Supported by dual borders: an outer structural line (`rgba(30, 27, 75, 0.08)`) and an inner soft rim highlight (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`). Shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(30, 27, 75, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(30, 27, 75, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Tactile Hover & Drag States):** Active clinical tags, treatment pins, and dragged patient appointments lift subtly with `transform: translateY(-1px)` and a targeted iris shadow: `0 8px 24px -2px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a refined balance between clinical precision and architectural softness.

- **Primary Geometry:** Default elements use a structured 8px radius (`0.5rem`). This provides modern warmth without compromising the rectilinear grid discipline necessary for complex medical matrices.
- **Cards & Panes (`rounded-lg`):** Main clinical containers, patient summary cards, and photographic viewports utilize a 16px radius (`1rem`), framing medical assets cleanly against the porcelain backdrop.
- **Status Pills & Micro-Controls:** Micro-badges, dosage chips, filter selectors, and interactive injection markers utilize full pill styling to stand out as distinct, touch-safe targets.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Clinical Action:** Background `#1E1B4B`, text `#FFFFFF`, radius `8px`, height `40px` (or `34px` compact). High-contrast label in `label-md`. Hover state subtly transitions to `#312E81`.
- **Secondary (Iris Interactive):** Background `rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.08)`, text `#6366F1`, border `1px solid rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.18)`. Hover transitions to solid Iris `#6366F1` with white text.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Borderless, deep slate text (`rgba(30, 27, 75, 0.7)`), background transparent, applying subtle porcelain hover wash (`rgba(30, 27, 75, 0.04)`).

### Cards & Clinical Containers
- Engineered with pure white foundations (`#FFFFFF`), rounded-lg corners, and dual low-contrast rims. 
- Headers feature sharp baseline dividing lines (`1px solid rgba(30, 27, 75, 0.04)`), separating patient demographic metadata from procedure workflows.

### Micro-Badges & Status Indicators
- **Verified / Paid:** Eucalyptus background (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.08)`), text `#059669`, border `1px solid rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.2)`. Uses `label-sm` with a `6px` solid status bead.
- **Pending Dues / Action Required:** Champagne Amber background (`rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.08)`), text `#D97706`, border `1px solid rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.2)`.
- **In-Treatment / Active:** Iris Violet background (`rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.08)`), text `#6366F1`, border `1px solid rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.2)`.

### Input Fields & Metric Selectors
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid rgba(30, 27, 75, 0.12)`, radius `8px`. Padding `10px 14px`. 
- Focus state eliminates standard blue rings; instead, it transitions to a precise `1px solid #6366F1` with an ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.12)`).
- Tabular numeric alignment applied to dosage and billing units.

### Checkboxes & Segmented Controls
- Segmented switches sit inside a recessed porcelain channel (`#F3F4F8`) with a sliding white pill indicator elevated by `Level 1` shadow.
- Checkboxes use an 8px softly squared form factor, turning `#1E1B4B` when selected, with a centered white geometric check.

### Additional Domain Components
- **Facial Mapping Nodes:** Scalable vector pins with Iris cores (`#6366F1`) and semi-transparent halos for designating Botulinum toxin units or dermal filler depth.
- **Comparative Photographic Slider:** Ultra-thin frosted divider bar (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`) with crisp split-pane before/after alignment labels.
